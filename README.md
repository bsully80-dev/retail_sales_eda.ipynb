Retail Sales EDA & Visual Storytelling

Exploratory data analysis of a retail sales dataset — cleaning, distribution analysis, and correlation testing to surface performance trends across product lines and city markets. Completed with 100% accuracy as part of a Data Science with Python course.

What This Project Does

Starting from a raw retail sales CSV (Product, Price, City, Date), this notebook:

Validates data quality — checks for nulls and duplicates before analysis
Cleans categorical data — standardizes product names (case, whitespace) to prevent duplicate categories from skewing results
Converts and prepares data types — parses dates for time-based analysis, calculates IQR to flag price outliers
Visualizes distributions and relationships — price distribution histogram, sales count by city, and a correlation heatmap across numeric fields
Translates findings into a business recommendation — not just charts, but an actionable takeaway
Key Finding

Delhi and Bengaluru emerged as the top-performing city markets by sales volume, while the data showed a downward trend in overall sales over the analyzed period — pointing to two concrete next steps: study what's driving success in the top markets to replicate it elsewhere, and investigate the cause of the broader decline.
